This page explains how to request deletion of your **Singlado** account and the
personal data associated with it.

## How to request deletion

Email **singlado.music@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your
Singlado account, with the subject line **"Delete my account"**.

Please include:

- the **email address** of the Singlado account you want deleted.

We verify the request comes from the account owner (we reply to the account
email), then process the deletion. We action verified requests within **30
days** and confirm by email when it's done.

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the personal data tied to it, including:

- your **account details** (email address and display name);
- your **playlists**, **favorite tracks and artists**, and **listening
  history**;
- your **sign-in credentials** (the stored password hash) and any linked
  sign-in identity (e.g. Google).

After deletion this data is permanently removed from our systems.

## What may be retained

We may retain a limited amount of data where we are required to for legal,
security, or fraud-prevention reasons (for example, minimal records of a
purchase for accounting/tax obligations). Such records are kept only as long as
required and are not used for any other purpose.

Purchases themselves (e.g. the one-time Premium unlock) are processed by Google
Play; any payment records held by Google are governed by Google's own policies.

## Questions

For anything about account or data deletion, contact
**singlado.music@gmail.com**. See also our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
