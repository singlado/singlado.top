Here's what you get.

## Playback that stays out of the way
- **A queue that builds itself.** Tap a track and Singlado fills the queue from
  whatever you were looking at, so you're not stopping after one song.
- **Shuffle and repeat.** Shuffle the lot, repeat one track, or repeat the whole
  queue.
- **Autoplay when the queue ends.** Instead of going silent, it keeps going with
  related tracks.
- **Controls where you need them.** Play, pause and skip from the notification,
  lock screen, headphones or car.
- **A seek bar you can trust.** It uses the real track length, so dragging it
  lands where you expect.

## Your library, wherever you sign in
- **Favorites.** One tap on the heart saves a track.
- **Playlists.** Build and reorder your own.
- **History.** Pick up where you left off.
- **Synced to your account.** Sign in on another device and it's all there.

## The look
- **Proper cover art.** Album artwork and metadata, not grey placeholders.
- **Dark theme with a green accent.** Easy on the eyes, built for listening.

## Signing in
- **Email and password**, or **Continue with Google**. Use either; they work
  side by side.

## Open by design
- **Nothing bundled.** Singlado is the player; the sources are **plugins** you
  add. Here's [how plugins work](/plugins).
- **Premium is a one-time purchase.** More on [Premium](/premium).
