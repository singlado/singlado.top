## Playback that just works
- **Context queue** — tap a track and Singlado builds the queue from what you
  were browsing, so the music keeps flowing.
- **Shuffle & repeat** — shuffle the queue, repeat one or repeat all.
- **Infinite autoplay** — when the queue runs out, Singlado keeps going with
  related tracks so the silence never falls.
- **Lock-screen & Bluetooth controls** — play, pause and skip from your
  notification, lock screen, headphones or car.
- **Accurate seek bar** — real track duration, so the progress bar tracks the
  music exactly.

## Your library, synced
- **Favorites** — tap the heart to save tracks you love.
- **Playlists** — build and organize your own playlists.
- **Listening history** — pick up where you left off.
- **Account sync** — your favorites, playlists and history follow you across
  devices.

## Looks right
- **Real cover art** — rich album artwork and metadata.
- **Dark, accent-aware theme** — a clean near-black UI with a green accent.

## Sign in your way
- **Email & password**, or **Continue with Google** — your choice. Both coexist.

## Open by design
- **No bundled catalog.** Singlado is a player; its sources are **plugins** you
