("Singlado", "the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and your choices.

## 1. Who we are
Singlado is a music streaming application. The app communicates with the
Singlado backend service hosted at `https://singlado.top`.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information (provided by you).**
- Email address and a display name, used to create and manage your account and
  to sign you in.
- Password: stored only as a salted cryptographic hash; we never store or see
  your plaintext password.
- If you sign in with Google ("Continue with Google"), we receive from Google a
  verified email address and your basic profile name. We do **not** receive your
  Google password.

**Content you create in the app (tied to your account).**
- Playlists you create, tracks and artists you mark as favorites, and your
  listening history. This lets your library and history sync across your devices.

**Purchase information.**
- If you buy the premium (ad‑free) upgrade, the purchase is processed by **Google
  Play Billing**. We receive a purchase token to activate your entitlement; we do
  **not** receive or store your payment card details.

**Advertising information.**
- The free version of the app shows ads via **Google AdMob**. AdMob may access
  your device's **advertising identifier (Advertising ID)** and related
  information to serve and measure ads. See Google's policies in Section 5.

**Technical information.**
- When the app contacts our backend, our servers process standard request data
  (such as IP address and app version) to operate and secure the service. We send
  account verification / one‑time codes by email through our email provider.

We do **not** collect your precise location, your contacts, your photos, or files
from your device.

## 3. How we use information
- To create and operate your account and sign you in.
- To provide core features: search, playback, playlists, favorites, and history
  sync.
- To process the optional premium purchase and apply your entitlement.
- To display and measure advertising in the free version.
- To secure the service, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

## 4. Legal basis & retention
We process account and content data to perform the service you request. We keep
your account data while your account exists; you may delete your account (see
Section 7), after which we delete or anonymize associated personal data, except
where we must retain limited records for legal or security reasons.

## 5. Third‑party services
Singlado relies on these providers, each with its own privacy policy:
- **Google Play Services / Google Sign‑In** — authentication.
- **Google Play Billing** — purchases.
- **Google AdMob** — advertising (uses the Advertising ID). Google's policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy . You can learn about ad personalization
  and opt out in your device settings (see Section 6).
- **Resend** — transactional email delivery (verification / one‑time codes).

## 6. Your choices
- **Ads / Advertising ID:** you can reset or limit your Advertising ID, and opt
  out of ad personalization, from your Android device settings
  (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying the premium upgrade removes ads.
- **Account data:** you can view and change your playlists, favorites, and
  history in the app.
- **Sign‑in method:** you may use email/password or Google.

## 7. Deleting your account and data
To delete your account and the personal data associated with it, contact
**singlado.music@gmail.com** from your account email, or use the in‑app account
deletion option where available. We will delete your account data within a
reasonable period, subject to the retention exceptions in Section 4.

## 8. Children
Singlado is not directed to children under the age required by your country's
law to consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect data from such
children.

## 9. Security
Passwords are hashed; traffic to our backend uses HTTPS. No method of
transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to
protect your information.

## 10. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will change. Material
changes will be communicated in the app or by email where appropriate.

## 11. Contact
Questions or requests: **singlado.music@gmail.com**
